Yoga Ball Collect — Glue5 Android port prototype

This is a Glue5/Luau-native, non-combat adaptation inspired by the idea of collecting balls.
It does not reproduce the original PC mod's NPC-death, weapon-loadout, or combat mechanics.

Menu (F7 → MENUS → Yoga Ball Collect):
- Start collecting
- Spawn 8 collectibles
- Stop collecting
- Clear collectibles
- Reset score

Important: this is a prototype and has not been runtime-tested on your phone. The chosen collectible model may not exist in every GTA V game build; if it fails, check the mod log in Glue5 Studio.
